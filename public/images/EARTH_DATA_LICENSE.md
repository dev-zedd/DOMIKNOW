# Earth journey geographic data

`earth-land.geojson` contains compact coordinate rings derived from Natural Earth
1:50m land and the regional 1:10m lake outlines. Nonregional coastlines are sampled
and coordinates rounded for a lightweight illustration. This is a discovery
illustration, not a survey or navigation map.

Natural Earth data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source datasets:
- https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson
- https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_lakes.geojson

Siniloan's camera target uses the application's existing service-area center:
14.42143 N, 121.44583 E.
