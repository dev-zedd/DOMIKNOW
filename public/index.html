Frequently asked questions

Know what happens before you continue.

Clear answers about listings, location access, accounts, applications, and verified feedback.

Browse rentals
What does a verified rental mean?

A verified rental has passed DOMIKNOW's administrative listing review. You should still read the complete property details, confirm the address, and review every lease term before committing.

Can I browse properties without creating an account?

Yes. Public visitors can browse the map, compare available listings, and open property details. An account is required when you are ready to apply and manage your rental journey.

Why does DOMIKNOW request my device location?

Location access powers the Near Me search and distance estimates. A successful reading is cached only in your browser session for up to 15 minutes. You can deny access and select a search point directly on the map instead.

Who can create a DOMIKNOW account?

Tenants and landlords can register through the public sign-up flow. Maintenance personnel receive their credentials directly from the landlord who manages their work assignments.

How do public reviews become visible?

Feedback must come from an authenticated tenant connected to an eligible lease. It is shown publicly only after it has been reviewed and marked visible, and tenant names are not displayed.

Where can I see the complete price and rental terms?

Open the property details page to review monthly rent, available spaces, deposits, amenities, house rules, and location information. The signed lease remains the final source of agreed rental terms.
